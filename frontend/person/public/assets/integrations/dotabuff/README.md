Downloaded on 2026-10-22. The linked Dotabuff item catalog could not be accessed during the design work.

Rank medals and star overlays were downloaded on 2026-10-23 from the [OpenDota web repository](https://github.com/odota/web/tree/master/public/assets/images/dota2/rank_icons):

- `ranks/rank-{tier}.png`: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odota/web/master/public/assets/images/dota2/rank_icons/rank_icon_{tier}.png`, tiers 0–8.
- `ranks/stars-{level}.png`: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odota/web/master/public/assets/images/dota2/rank_icons/rank_star_{level}.png`, levels 1–5.

The medal and its star overlay retain the game's original artwork and are layered locally to show the exact rank level. Valve retains artwork and trademark rights.
